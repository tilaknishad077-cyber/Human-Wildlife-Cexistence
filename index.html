Safety Protocol Guide

What to do during a tiger encounter

  • • Stop and move backward slowly.
  • • Keep your face visible and avoid eye contact.
  • • Make yourself appear larger and call for help.
  • • Do not run or climb unstable structures.

Safe grazing times & perimeter caution

  • • Graze only between 6 AM and 4 PM.
  • • Stay away from the forest edge and dried shrubs.
  • • Use one designated watch point.
  • • Avoid lone grazing after sunset.

Securing livestock at night

  • • Lock cattle in a sturdy enclosure by 6 PM.
  • • Keep bright lights and watchers near pens.
  • • Avoid leaving fodder outside overnight.
  • • Report unusual movements immediately.
